# Research journals

The working context of the Delphi evaluation campaign, kept as written so the
record shows what was known when each decision was made.

- [`STATE.md`](STATE.md): runs, measured results, blockers, and next triggers,
  round by round.
- [`DECISIONS.md`](DECISIONS.md): methodological decisions and why they were made.
- [`HYPOTHESES.md`](HYPOTHESES.md): candidate product improvements, expected
  effects, disconfirmers, and ablations.
- [`EXPLORATION.md`](EXPLORATION.md): what was inspected and what the evidence
  said at the time.
- [`EXPOSURE_LEDGER.md`](EXPOSURE_LEDGER.md): the case-level exposure ledger and
  the round chronology behind Appendix C of the paper.
- [`PILOT_PROTOCOL.md`](PILOT_PROTOCOL.md): the preregistered executable pilot.
- [`ARTIFACT_INVENTORY.md`](ARTIFACT_INVENTORY.md): the inputs of every table,
  with SHA-256 digests.
- Workstreams: [`DELPHI_WORKSTREAM.md`](DELPHI_WORKSTREAM.md),
  [`ROUND4_WORKSTREAM.md`](ROUND4_WORKSTREAM.md),
  [`FILE_OKAPI_WORKSTREAM.md`](FILE_OKAPI_WORKSTREAM.md),
  [`NIA_WORKSTREAM.md`](NIA_WORKSTREAM.md),
  [`NIA_FRONTIER_AUDIT.md`](NIA_FRONTIER_AUDIT.md),
  [`CONTEXT7_WORKSTREAM.md`](CONTEXT7_WORKSTREAM.md),
  [`CONTEXT7_EXTENSION.md`](CONTEXT7_EXTENSION.md).

These files mention paths that no longer exist in the working tree. The
round-3 research blog (`blog/`), its vignette tooling, the comparison canvases,
the File-Okapi design docs, the ICLR-anonymous paper draft, and the raw pilot
trajectories were removed in the post-submission cleanup; all of them remain in
git history at commit `bdc21bc`, and the trajectories are published at
<https://huggingface.co/datasets/aayambansall/delphi-benchmark-traces>.
No API keys, credentials, or frozen-final labels are recorded here.
